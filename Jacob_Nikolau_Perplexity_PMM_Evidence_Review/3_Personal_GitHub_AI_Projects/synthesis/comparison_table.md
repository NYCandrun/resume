## Calibration notes

- Ratings for research pipelines (chip, Moondream, airops) and for app prototypes (marketplace_prototype, etta_app_update) use different bars. "Strong" for a pipeline means its own gates were demonstrably exercised and caught real faults. "Solid" for a prototype means tests and build pass and at least one independent review loop is visible. None of these ratings implies production readiness.
- Where investigators disagreed (for example etta_app_update "CI green" in June vs the July defects), synthesis took the July commit `be2d460` as evidence that the June gates were insufficient. The rating is Moderate rather than Solid.
- The research-prompt lineage (ai_game kit → airops → research_prompt → genesis) is one body of work. Its four rows are not four independent demonstrations.
