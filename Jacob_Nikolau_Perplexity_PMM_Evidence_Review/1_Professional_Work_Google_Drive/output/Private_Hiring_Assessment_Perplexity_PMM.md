## 1. Recommendation

**Interview: yes.**

He is a credible product marketer. His record is strongest on research-led positioning, AI product storytelling, launch-readiness kits and analytical candor.

**What sets him apart (supported by evidence):**
1. **Research-to-positioning chain**, visible end to end:
   - Cascade ROI study with 127 participants plus 6 IDIs; Jacob is the named preparer (deck, slide 9).
   - That study produced six value drivers (one-pager) and the feature taxonomy.
   - Three public surveys: 515 leaders (2022), 505 professionals (2023) and 138 A&E professionals (2026). Jacob is the named co-author of the 2026 [report](https://www.mosaicapp.com/whitepapers/state-of-ai-in-a-e-2026).
2. **Claim discipline on an AI product.** The Chello BDR talk track labels every capability LIVE · JUNE 2026 or ROADMAP Q3/Q4 2026. This maps directly to positioning an answer engine where accuracy is the brand.
3. **Launch kits as systems.** For Lesson Libraries he co-bylined the user stories with the PM, and the kit also includes a phased FAQ, early-adopter scoring, an enablement deck and a CX email. He also wrote a draft release-prep process with launch tiers and an audience-by-enablement matrix.
4. **Data-grounded narratives that include bad news.** He produced a cost-per-SAO channel audit and a LinkedIn paid review that candidly reported weak paid-channel results.
5. **AI-native operator.** He generated workbooks with Claude, turned AI-assisted deep research into a 13-segment playbook, and built Claude skills on RevOps' Gong/Salesforce plugin. He usually labels AI-assisted work as such.

**Fit consideration:**
1. **Tenure.** The posting asks for 8+ years of PMM (see section 2).

**Coverage:** about 150 of roughly 1,980 work-period Drive files were opened and about 35 read in depth. That is a targeted sample.
