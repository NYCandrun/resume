## Strong examples (problem → decision → artifact → execution → outcome)

- **NovoEd value narrative.** The problem was weak proof of customer value. The decision was to commission ROI research (Cascade, n=127 + 6 IDIs), which produced six value drivers, the Value Drivers one-pager (2024), and taxonomy alignment.
- **Mosaic A&E market POV.** The problem was a generic "AEC" pitch. The decision was to segment into 13 sub-industries and publish primary research (n=138, Jacob as co-author). The artifacts are the report and the SDR signal checklist, with the talk track gating claims by LIVE/ROADMAP. A second-wave draft exists.
- **BitcoinComputer technical storytelling.** The problem was explaining a novel primitive. The decision was to anchor it to familiar tools: "JavaScript smart contracts for Bitcoin… No trusted 3rd parties". The artifacts are the site IA organized by evaluation stage and the zkSync teardown.
